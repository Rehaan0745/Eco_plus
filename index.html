The Closed-Loop Bio-Ecosystem

How plant-derived materials move through the production lifecycle.
01

Raw Corn Cultivation

Natural corn crops provide the renewable organic base material used in the production process.

02

Corn Starch Extraction

Corn elements undergo processing to isolate the starch component used as a plant-based input.

03

Polymer Synthesis

Starch-based inputs are processed into compostable PLA and PBAT material structures.

04

Bio Polymer Manufacturing

Material granules are processed through production lines to form practical packaging products.

05

End-of-Life Decomposition

Compostable products are intended to return to natural biological cycles under suitable conditions.
